Privacy policy

Draft for review. This plain-language draft describes how the website works. Please have it reviewed before launch and confirm the business details.

What we collect

If you submit the enquiry form, we receive the details you enter: your name, mobile number, optional email, area, requirement, electricity bill range and message.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry and to follow up about it. Submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet that only Solar Point staff can open.

Sharing

We do not sell your details. Your enquiry is processed using Google services (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) and the website is delivered through Cloudflare.

Contacting us on WhatsApp or phone

If you choose to call or message us on WhatsApp, those services' own privacy terms apply.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your enquiry by contacting us using the details on the contact page.
